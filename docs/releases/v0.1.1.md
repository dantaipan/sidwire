## Updating

- Replace "SID Bridge.app" in Applications with the new one.
- For the plug-ins, replace "SID Bridge.component" and "SID Bridge.vst3" in your plug-in folders, then restart your DAW or rescan its plug-ins.

Your settings, profiles and presets stay as they are.
