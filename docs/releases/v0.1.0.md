The first release of SID Bridge: play the real SID chips in a C64 Ultimate from a MIDI keyboard or your DAW, over your network. Nothing runs on the C64 and no MIDI cartridge is needed.

## Download

**[SID-Bridge-0.1.0.dmg](https://github.com/dantaipan/sid-bridge/releases/download/v0.1.0/SID-Bridge-0.1.0.dmg)**: the standalone app, the Audio Unit and the VST3, for macOS 13 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel). Signed and notarized. Open it and follow "Read Me".

## What's in it

- **Standalone app and AU / VST3 instrument plugin.** Setup finds your C64U on the network by itself; Play has the sound in four sections (oscillator, filter, envelope, output), the voice modes and the voices.
- **Seven voice modes:** poly, mono, unison, octave, fifth, chord, and an arpeggiator (1/4 to 1/32 with triplets, five patterns, 1-3 octaves, gate) that follows MIDI clock or your DAW's tempo.
- **Your controller:** controller profiles (generic keyboard and M-Audio Oxygen 49 MKV included) and MIDI learn: right-click a knob, move a control.
- **Stream C64U audio** plays the C64U's audio out of the plugin, so a DAW can record it.
- Velocity response, presets (your own presets file too), Silence, and an update check.

## Before you start

- Switch on **Web Remote Control** and, for the lowest latency, **Ultimate DMA Service** in the C64U menu under Network Settings.
- Allow SID Bridge (or your DAW) to find devices on your local network when macOS asks.
- What to expect from latency: see the [README](https://github.com/dantaipan/sid-bridge#latency-what-to-expect).

The Python bridge (`bridge.py`) is in the source code.
