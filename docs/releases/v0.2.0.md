SID Bridge is now **SIDwire**. Same app, new name: "C64 Bridge" is a different tool for the C64 Ultimate, and the names were too close.

## Download

**[SIDwire-0.2.0.dmg](https://github.com/dantaipan/sidwire/releases/download/v0.2.0/SIDwire-0.2.0.dmg)**: the standalone app, the Audio Unit and the VST3, for macOS 13 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel). Signed and notarized. Open it and follow "Read Me".

## Upgrading from SID Bridge

1. Install SIDwire from the disk image as usual.
2. Delete the old copies: **SID Bridge.app** from Applications, and **SID Bridge.component** and **SID Bridge.vst3** from your plug-in folders (/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and /VST3). With both installed your DAW may load the old one. If any are left, SIDwire's Setup tab says so and can show them in Finder.
3. Restart your DAW or rescan its plug-ins.

What carries over by itself:

- **DAW projects:** SIDwire has the same plug-in identity (AU codes and VST3 class ID), so in most DAWs (Logic, Live, Bitwig, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper) projects made with SID Bridge open with SIDwire and keep their sound and settings. A host that remembers a VST3 by its file path instead may say "SID Bridge.vst3" is missing: rescan its plug-ins and load SIDwire in that slot, or use the AU.
- **Settings, controller profiles (including your MIDI learn copies) and update choices** are copied from SID Bridge on the first run.
